BodySync Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Developer: Shubham

Contact: support@bodysync.app

BodySync ("the app") is a health and wellness app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

1. The short version

  • BodySync has no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Your health information is stored on your phone. We do not receive it, and we do not upload it to any server.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. Information the app handles on your phone

You choose what to enter. The app stores this on your phone only:

  • Profile details: your name, birth year, sex, height and weight. These are used to estimate calories and walking distance, and to check that you are 18 or older.
  • Activity: daily and hourly step counts read from your phone's step sensor, and goals you set.
  • Water: the amounts you log.
  • Sleep: the sleep, bedtime and wake-up times you log or set.
  • Cycle tracking: period days, symptoms and notes you log.
  • Medicine reminders: the times you choose. The app does not save medicine names.
  • Cycling rides: date, duration, distance, speed, an estimated calorie figure and the GPS route of each ride you record.
  • Routines and settings: for example, completed stretch or breathing sessions, reminder settings and your preferred units.

3. Location

The app uses your precise location only while you are recording a ride, to measure distance and draw your route. A notification is shown while a ride is being recorded. The app does not track your location when you are not recording a ride. Location is obtained through Google Play services. We do not send your location to anyone.

4. Maps and the internet

When you open a ride's map, the app downloads map pictures (tiles) from OpenStreetMap servers. Those servers can see your IP address and which map area you are viewing. See the OpenStreetMap privacy policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. The app does not use the internet for anything else.

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Physical activity: to count your steps.
  • Notifications: to show reminders and the step-counting notification.
  • Location (precise): to record cycling rides.
  • Foreground service: to keep counting steps and to record rides while the screen is off.
  • Run at startup: to restart step counting after your phone restarts.
  • Set alarm: to open your Clock app with a wake-up alarm when you ask for one.
  • Internet: to download map tiles.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose your information to anyone, because we do not receive it. The only third-party service involved is OpenStreetMap (map tiles), described above.

7. Backups

BodySync is set so that its data is not included in Google cloud backup. If you transfer your data directly to a new phone using Android's phone-to-phone transfer, the app's data may be copied to the new phone.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete individual rides, delete all cycle data from the Cycle settings, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data.

9. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by Android's security. The app does not add its own encryption, so we recommend that you protect your phone with a screen lock.

10. Not medical advice

BodySync is not a medical device. Calorie figures and cycle predictions are estimates. The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and cycle predictions must not be used for birth control. Medicine reminders are reminders only.

11. Children

BodySync is for people aged 18 and over. It is not intended for children, and the app asks for your birth year to prevent use by anyone under 18.

12. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if ads are added), we will update this policy and the effective date above.

13. Contact
